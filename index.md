# The Aviary — Support

The Aviary is a cozy game about discovering the birds around you by their sound: record a bird's
song, the app identifies it right on your phone, and the bird comes to live in your own little forest.

## Get help

Questions, bug reports, or feedback — email us and we'll get back to you:

**[hgonzembach@gmail.com](mailto:hgonzembach@gmail.com)**

## Common questions

**Does the app upload my recordings?** No. Bird identification runs entirely on your device and your
recordings never leave your phone. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

**Why does the app ask for my location?** Only an approximate area is used, to know which birds are
expected near you and to flag unusual sightings. You can decline and still play.
